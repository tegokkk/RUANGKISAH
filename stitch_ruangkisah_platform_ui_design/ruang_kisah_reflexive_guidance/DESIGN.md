## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a sanctuary for self-reflection and guidance (Bimbingan Konseling) tailored specifically for Indonesian high school students. It pairs editorial poise with adolescent psychological safety. Instead of gamified loops, dopamine-driven counters, or clinical health aesthetics, the interface communicates warm maturity, unconditional privacy, and grounded calm.

The overarching aesthetic blends **Editorial Modernism** with **Soft Tactility**. Broad visual canvases, intentional typography, generous breathing room, and soft pill shapes evoke the feeling of a curated personal notebook rather than a corporate utility or institutional reporting tool.

Key brand tenets include:
- **Zero-Surveillance Privacy**: Absolute avoidance of profile avatars, user logins, and cloud-sync statuses. All reflections are local-first, ephemeral, or explicitly self-saved.
- **Non-Judgmental Emotion Handling**: Visual neutrality across all emotional states. Emotional reflections avoid binary, value-laden chromatic signals (no "red for bad" or "green for good").
- **Focused Reflection**: Minimal visual distraction in active self-inquiry spaces, constraining reading and writing widths to comfortable, distraction-free dimensions.

## Colors

The palette balances daylight warmth with grounding earthen depth. It avoids harsh stark whites and pure digital blacks in favor of organic tones.

- **Primary Canvas & Surface**: The foundation rests on warm off-white canvas (`#F7F8F2`) layered with crisp `#FFFFFF` cards and modules for clear content separation.
- **Dark Ink (`#182420`)**: Serves as the high-contrast foundational neutral for primary text, active monoline iconography, and dedicated focal sections (such as the landing hero container). It is not a site-wide dark mode, but a deliberate grounding surface.
- **Pistachio (`#B9C255`) & Frost (`#E6F1FA`)**: Secondary daylight tones. Pistachio acts as an inviting accent on dark hero sections, highlight cards, and guidance banners. Frost acts as an open, cool tint for reflection modules, privacy indicators, and selected emotion chips.
- **Iced Denim (`#5798D7`)**: Applied as an accent tone and soft illustration canvas. To ensure accessible contrast, text placed atop Iced Denim must always use Dark Ink (`#182420`), never white.
- **Action Blue (`#245F94`)**: The principal interactive driver on light surfaces for primary pill buttons, focus outlines, and persistent hyperlinks.

### Strict Contrast Rules
- Never pair white text over Pistachio (`#B9C255`) or Iced Denim (`#5798D7`). Both require Dark Ink (`#182420`).
- Emotion selection chips in reflective journaling modules must maintain uniform visual weight using Frost (`#E6F1FA`) and Dark Ink (`#182420`) to eliminate emotional bias.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on `Manrope` with clean geometric fallbacks (`Arial, sans-serif`). Script, cursive, or novelty handwriting fonts are explicitly forbidden to preserve legibility and emotional neutrality.

- **Editorial Rhythm**: Display headings leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to establish an authoritative, thoughtful editorial appearance.
- **Reading Comfort**: Body copy is set at generous line heights (`26px` to `28px`) to ensure reading flow remains unhurried and accessible on both mobile displays and desktop viewports.
- **Copy Voice & Language**: All user-facing copy uses gentle, respectful, and direct natural Bahasa Indonesia (e.g., *"Ceritakan apa yang sedang terasa"*, *"Langkah kecil untuk hari ini"*). Technical jargon, psychological diagnoses, or juvenile slang are systematically avoided.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured layout framework balances modular content blocks with comfortable writing chambers:

- **Desktop (1440px target)**: 12-column grid with a centered max container width of `1120px` and `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px target)**: 8-column grid with `32px` page margins and `20px` gutters.
- **Mobile (390px target, testing down to 320px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `20px` margins (collapsing to `16px` on narrow screens) and `16px` gutters. Content reflows into a single primary column.
- **The Journaling Sanctuary**: Active journal inputs, multi-step reflection questionnaires, and guidance reading containers are strictly capped at `720px` max-width desktop (`680px` tablet) to maintain an intimate writing scale.
- **Vertical Spacing Scale**: Section gaps range between `64px`–`80px` on desktop and scale gracefully down to `40px`–`48px` on mobile screens to preserve breathing space between thematic modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile surface shifts rather than dramatic digital drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers & Boundaries**: Depth is achieved by placing stark pure-white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted surface containers (`#E6F1FA`, `#B9C255`) against the warmer `#F7F8F2` canvas. Cards use crisp, subtle borders (`1px solid #DCE4DE`) instead of heavy shadows.
- **Tactile Hover Behavior**: Interactive cards maintain low-altitude elevation, responding to hover with a maximum upward translation of `-2px` and an imperceptible diffused ambient tint (`0 4px 12px rgba(24, 36, 32, 0.04)`).
- **Dialog & Modal Scrims**: Modal depth employs an ink scrim (`#182420` at 40% opacity) that obscures background content to heighten focus. Modals sit atop this layer with an elevation shadow (`0 12px 32px rgba(24, 36, 32, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape system combines large, inviting corner fillets with fully rounded pill elements to cultivate an approachable and soft aesthetic:

- **Large Structural Canvas & Hero Panels**: Sized with `32px` corner radii on desktop, gracefully adapting to `24px` on mobile viewports.
- **Cards, Content Panels, and Modals**: Styled with uniform `24px` radii (`20px` on mobile devices).
- **Form Controls & Text Areas**: Configured with `16px` corner radii for a friendly, modern container silhouette that remains distinctly rectangular.
- **Pill Primitives**: All buttons, status chips, segmented emotion toggles, and metadata badges utilize complete pill radii (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons (`C-02`)
- **Primary Pill (Light Canvas)**: Minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`. Solid fill in Action Blue (`#245F94`) with white typography (`#FFFFFF`). Hover state deepens to `#1B4C78`.
- **Primary Pill (Dark Hero Surface)**: Solid Pistachio (`#B9C255`) fill with Dark Ink (`#182420`) typography.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Transparent fill, `1px solid #718178` (Border Control), Dark Ink (`#182420`) typography.
- **Destructive Parity**: Equal visual weight for non-saving actions (e.g., *"Selesai tanpa simpan"*) vs. saving actions (*"Simpan di perangkat"*), preventing dark patterns or coercion.

### Interactive Emotion Chips (`C-06`)
- **Dimensions & Radius**: Fully pill-shaped, minimum height `44px` with `16px` horizontal padding.
- **Resting State**: Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #DCE4DE` border and Dark Ink (`#182420`) text accompanied by neutral monoline outline icons (1.75px stroke).
- **Selected State**: Tinted with Frost (`#E6F1FA`), a `2px solid #245F94` perimeter border, without color-coding based on emotion value.

### Text Inputs & Reflection Textareas (`C-05`)
- **Geometry**: `16px` border radius; textareas maintain a minimum vertical height of `160px` desktop (`144px` mobile).
- **Resting Border**: `1px solid #718178` on pure `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Active Focus**: `2px solid #245F94` without structural layout shift, reinforced with an external `3px` focus ring offset by `3px`.
- **Helper Copy**: Secondary helper labels and character counters are set in Muted Text (`#52645C`) at 14px.

### Cards & Content Modules
- **Modular Cards**: Set on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or Frost (`#E6F1FA`), bordered with `1px solid #DCE4DE` and `24px` corner radii. Internal padding is `32px` on desktop and scales to `20px` on mobile.
- **Specialized Privacy Card ("Catatan Saya")**: Strictly displays the module name and completion date. To safeguard student privacy from shoulder surfing in shared school environments, dynamic excerpts, titles, and emotional tags are deliberately omitted from record list items.

### Dialogs & Confirmation Modals (`C-13`)
- Max-width `480px` on desktop, adapting to full width with `16px` side margins on mobile.
- Enclosed with a `24px` radius, white surface, and accompanied by distinct, symmetrically weighted decision buttons.

### Abstract 3D & Illustrative Language
- Abstract 3D objects (folded ribbons, organic leaves, sprouts) must feature matte finishes. Gloss, chrome, neon, and high-specular reflective treatments are prohibited.
- Hero visuals are docked to the right of text blocks (7:5 ratio) on desktop, collapsing to an unobtrusive max height of `180px` on mobile. Form and writing pages omit decorative illustrations to preserve cognitive clarity.
